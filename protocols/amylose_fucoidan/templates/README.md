# Amylose-26 / Fucoidan-12 protocol templates

These files are **protocol templates** for automation smoke tests and
scientific-workflow wiring. They are **not** a crystal structure, a docked
pose, or evidence of a binding mechanism.

| ID | Chemistry | Charge |
|----|-----------|--------|
| `amylose_26` | 26 × α-D-Glcp, α-(1→4), left-handed V-type helix | 0 |
| `fucoidan_12` | 12 × α-L-Fucp, alternating α-(1→3)/α-(1→4), C2 sulfate on every residue | −12 |
| `fucoidan_12_alt_sulfation` | Same backbone; C2 on all 12 plus C4 on even residues | −18 |

Residue names (`AGLC`, `AFUS`), glycosidic connectivity, and net charges are
internally consistent and asserted by `tests/test_md_carb.py`. Coordinates
were generated by `scripts/build_templates.py` with CHARMM36 carbohydrate
atom types from the pinned MacKerell July 2022 GROMACS port. Sulfated
fucose uses carb methylsulfate types `OC30P` (ester O), `SC` (S), and
`OC2DP` (terminal =O) so `gmx grompp` can look up C2/C4–O–S bonds in
`ffbonded.itp`. Do not use CGenFF `OG303` on pyranose `CC3161`.

Do not let a model rewrite these files at runtime.
